## The Four Core Judging Criteria
Every track is judged on the exact same four foundational criteria:

1. **Technical Depth**:
   - Quality and complexity of engineering, code architecture, robustness, and effective use of advanced technologies (e.g. edge models, agent loops, eBPF, ZK proofs, smart contracts).
2. **Problem Innovation**:
   - Originality of approach, tackling genuine pain points, and avoiding generic wrappers or superficial ideas.
3. **Design & UX Craft**:
   - Interface polish, intuitive user flows, attention to detail, accessibility, and visual execution.
4. **Live Demo Quality**:
   - Working software demonstration, live functionality without smoke and mirrors, and clear presentation of results.

## Mentors & Judges
- 9 domain seats across AI, Web3, FinTech, HealthTech, CyberSecurity, and Open Innovation.
- Mentors will be physically present on the floor at GNIT throughout the 8-hour sprint to support teams with live debugging, architecture reviews, and pitch coaching.
