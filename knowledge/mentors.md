## Mentor Office Hours
- Mentors will be available throughout Saturday, October 17 from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM EST, and Sunday morning from 9:00 AM to 11:30 AM EST.
- Mentors can assist with architecture, debugging, cloud deployment, and pitch advice.

## Requesting a Mentor
- To request assistance, post a message in the `#mentor-request` channel using the following format:
  - Team Name
  - Brief description of the issue / tech stack
  - Voice channel link or thread
- An available mentor will claim the ticket and join your discussion.
