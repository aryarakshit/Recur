# RECURSIVE 2026 — Official Rules & Guidelines
*Updated: 2026-09-19*
*Source: https://recursiveacm.in*

## Team Size & Composition
- **Team Size**: Teams must consist of a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4 members (2–4 builders).
- **Solo Participation**: **Strictly prohibited**. Solo applications are not permitted.
- If you do not have a team yet, you can team up with fellow participants before registration closes or connect with other builders in our Discord community (`https://discord.gg/SMYB7tJQf`) to form your squad.

## Project Originality & Code Policy
- **No Pre-existing Code**: All code and designs must be created entirely during the official 8-hour hacking window. Writing core application code beforehand is strictly prohibited.
- You are encouraged to arrive with ideas, sketches, and architecture plans.
- You may use open-source libraries, public/third-party APIs (e.g. Gemini, Groq, OpenAI), and starter frameworks, but the core implementation must be built during the sprint.

## What to Bring on Event Day
- Laptop and charging brick.
- Power strip / extension cord for your team table.
- Valid College ID Card (or government photo ID: Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, passport, or driving licence).
- Any specific hardware components, microcontrollers, or sensors your project requires.
- Note: High-speed campus Wi-Fi, dedicated power stations, meals, snacks, beverages, and swag kits are provided free of cost.

## Code of Conduct
- Maintain an inclusive, collaborative, and respectful environment.
- Harassment, discrimination, offensive language, or unauthorized copying/plagiarism will result in immediate disqualification and removal from the venue.
