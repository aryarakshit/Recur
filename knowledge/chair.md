## The Lore Behind the Hackathon
*"Bro put a plastic chair on a hill and called it a hackathon."*

The plastic chair is an everyday staple across Kolkata, scattered on college terraces, roadside tea stalls, and neighborhood corners where people gather to talk for hours.

For RECURSIVE 2026, four of these chairs sit together on the hill as an open table for your team.

You arrive with people you know or team up in the morning, claim your spot, and spend eight focused hours turning an idea into working software before the day ends.
