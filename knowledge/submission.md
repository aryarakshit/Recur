# RECURSIVE 2026 — Application & Idea Submission Process
*Updated: 2026-09-19*
*Source: https://recursiveacm.in*

## How to Apply
Applications for Recursive are handled through Devfolio with an initial idea review round:

1. **Register on Devfolio**:
   - Apply at the official Devfolio page: [https://recursiveacm.devfolio.co](https://recursiveacm.devfolio.co)
   - Complete your personal profile.
   - Form a team of 2 to 4 members (minimum 2, maximum 4 builders). Solo participation is not permitted.
2. **Download the Official Idea Submission Template**:
   - Access the official [Recursive ACM Idea Submission Template](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Heaa2d_DUVpFmt4Oo2dKZWUOvAVtXQrn1OnsHCBEWEQ/copy) on Google Slides.
3. **Prepare Your Proposal**:
   - Follow the mandatory 8-slide deck structure outlined below.
4. **Export as PDF & Upload**:
   - Remove the final Guidelines slide (Slide 9).
   - Export your presentation as a PDF (maximum 8 slides total, including the cover slide).
   - Upload the PDF directly into your Devfolio application form before the deadline.
5. **Review & RSVP**:
   - The organizing committee reviews all submissions.
   - Shortlisted teams receive an acceptance invitation on Devfolio and via email to confirm attendance (RSVP) for the in-person event at GNIT.

## Official 8-Slide PPT Structure
Your deck must strictly follow this sequence:
- **Slide 1 — Cover Slide**: Shift-8 Recursive Hackathon branding with GNIT & ACM chapter headers.
- **Slide 2 — Team Details**: Team Name, chosen Theme/Track, and member table (Team Lead, Member 1, Member 2, Member 3: Full Name, College Name, Contact Number, Email).
- **Slide 3 — Problem Statement**: Identify and state a real-world problem, target users, why it matters, and existing gaps.
- **Slide 4 — Solution**: Clear description of idea/prototype, core features, how it solves the problem, uniqueness, and impact.
- **Slide 5 — Tech Stack**: Frontend, Backend, Database, AI/ML, reasons for choices, visual architecture/workflow diagram.
- **Slide 6 — Methodology**: Step-by-step workflow, key processes/algorithms, and flowchart.
- **Slide 7 — Feasibility & Scalability**: Technical/practical feasibility, resources/costs, and scalability potential.
- **Slide 8 — Business Impact & Future Scope**: Economic value, productivity gains, upcoming features, and future expansion.

## Critical PPT Guidelines
1. Maximum 8 slides (including cover slide).
2. Follow slide structure strictly without altering locked template elements.
3. Keep content concise; prefer diagrams, flowcharts, and architecture visuals over walls of text.
4. Solutions must be 100% original.
5. Remove Slide 9 (Guidelines) before final export.
6. Submit strictly in PDF format.
