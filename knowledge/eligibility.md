# RECURSIVE 2026 — Eligibility & Free Participation
*Updated: 2026-09-19*
*Source: https://recursiveacm.in*

## Who is Eligible?
- Open to any **undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma student** from any accredited institution or college.
- Beginners are warmly welcomed; mentors will be on the floor throughout the event to guide participants through architecture, coding, and debugging.

## Identification Requirements
- Participants must carry a **valid college ID card**.
- If unable to present a college ID, acceptable government photo IDs include:
  - Aadhaar card
  - PAN card
  - Voter ID
  - Passport
  - Driving licence

## 100% Free of Cost
- **Registration Fee**: INR 0 (Completely Free).
- Shortlisted participants receive free of cost:
  - Event admission & table seating
  - High-speed campus Wi-Fi
  - Meals, snacks, tea, and beverages
  - Exclusive hackathon swag kits
  - Mentorship and workshop access

## Team Size Requirements
- Minimum: 2 members
- Maximum: 4 members
- **Solo participation is strictly not permitted**.
