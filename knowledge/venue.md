## Transit & Getting to the Venue
- **By Train (Local Suburban)**:
  - Sodepur Railway Station (Sealdah North Line) is located approximately 1.2 km from campus.
  - Travel time from Sodepur station to GNIT is ~5 minutes via auto-rickshaw or e-rickshaw (toto).
- **By Bus / Road**:
  - Frequent direct buses operate via B.T. Road to the Sodepur crossing or directly along Nilgunj Road past the campus.
- **Drop-off & Parking**:
  - Direct drop-off is available right at the GNIT Main Gate.
  - Designated event vehicle parking is arranged on campus for participants and guests.

## On-Campus Facilities Provided
- High-speed campus Wi-Fi access for all builders.
- Dedicated team tables with power stations.
- Full catering: breakfast, lunch, snacks, water, and beverages throughout the 8-hour sprint.
- Mentor desks on the floor for live debugging and technical guidance.
